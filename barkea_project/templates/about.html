The Concept

Barkea Resort is designed as a modern residential community that blends contemporary architecture with the natural beauty of Lundër / Farkë. The project offers a peaceful and secure lifestyle, with spacious villas, green areas and premium infrastructure.

Architectural Language

The architecture follows a modern and minimalist style, with clean lines, natural materials and large openings that connect the interior with the surrounding nature.

Construction Standards & Materials

We use high-quality materials and modern construction standards to ensure durability, safety and long-term value. The project meets European standards for energy efficiency and sustainability.

Energy Efficiency & Thermal Insulation

Each villa is designed with advanced thermal insulation systems, energy-efficient windows and modern heating/cooling solutions for maximum comfort and lower energy consumption.

Our Developer

Barkea Resort is developed by a trusted real estate company with years of experience in residential projects, ensuring quality, transparency and on-time delivery.
